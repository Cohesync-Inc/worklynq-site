Features

Order Tracking

Follow every customer order from the moment it comes in to the moment it’s picked up, delivered, or shipped. Everyone can see what’s due, who’s on it, and what’s running late — so nobody has to ask twice.

What it’s for

  • Catering and cake orders at a café or bakery, special orders at a shop, repairs to hand back, parts to ship.
  • A board with a column for each stage, so you can see the whole day at a glance.
  • Items, prices, and a total; pickup, delivery, shipping, or in store; a due time; and who is on it.
  • A history of who did what and when, on every order.

Order Tracking is part of Customer Experience and uses your customer records, so every order belongs to a customer.

Turn on Order Tracking

  • It’s on from the start for Operations businesses (restaurants, cafés, retail, hospitality) and for Software businesses.
  • For anyone else, your System Admin turns it on in Admin console → Work Configuration → Modules. It needs the Customers module, which is turned on with it.
  • Once it’s on, Order Tracking appears in the sidebar.

Add an order

  1. Open Order Tracking and click New order.
  2. Pick an existing customer, or add a new customer with a name, phone, and email.
  3. Add the items with a quantity and price. Worklynq works out the total.
  4. Choose how it reaches them: Pickup, Delivery, Shipping, or In store.
  5. Set the due time — Ready by, Deliver by, or Ship by, depending on how it reaches them.
  6. Optionally add who it’s assigned to, a delivery address, carrier and tracking number, a reference, and notes. Then click Create order.

Orders you add on a customer’s record show up here too, with their own order number.

How an order moves

New→Confirmed→In progress→Ready→On the way→ CompletedCancelled
  • Pickup and in-store orders skip On the way — they go straight from Ready to Completed.
  • Any order can be cancelled, and a completed or cancelled order can be reopened.

The board and the list

The Board has one column for each open stage: New, Confirmed, In progress, Ready, and On the way.

  • Drag a card to another column, or use its Move to … button.
  • Overdue orders are outlined in red.
  • The board refreshes every minute while it’s open, so a screen in the kitchen or at the counter stays current.

The List shows open orders, completed and cancelled ones, or all orders.

Filters work on both: search by order number, customer, item, phone, or tracking number; filter by pickup, delivery, or shipping; and by due time — Due today, Overdue, or Upcoming.

Inside an order

Click any order to open it. You’ll see:

  • A stage bar — click any stage to move the order there.
  • The next step as one button, like Move to Ready or Mark completed, plus Cancel and Reopen.
  • The order’s details and items, which you can edit.
  • The history: every change, who made it, when, and any note.

Orders and customers

Each customer’s record lists their orders, with a Track link that opens the order here. Orders from before Order Tracking was turned on get an order number and a stage the first time they’re opened or edited — nothing else about them changes.

Tips

  • Set a due time on every order — it’s what makes Due today and the red overdue outline work.
  • Assign each order to someone, so the board shows who’s on it.
  • Add the carrier and tracking number on shipped orders; you can then search by tracking number.
