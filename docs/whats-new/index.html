What’s new

New features and improvements in Worklynq, newest first.

October 25, 2026

New

Order Tracking

A new Customer Experience module for following customer orders from New to Completed. Add items and prices, choose pickup, delivery, shipping, or in store, set a due time, and assign it to someone. The board has a column for each stage, overdue orders are outlined in red, and every order keeps a history of who did what. It’s on from the start for Operations and Software businesses; anyone else can turn it on in the Admin console.

Read about Order Tracking →

New

Use Worklynq from Claude and ChatGPT

Add Worklynq as a connector in Claude (claude.ai, desktop, or mobile) or ChatGPT, sign in, and click Allow — no API token needed. You choose whether the app may edit tickets, docs, and dashboards, and you can see and disconnect your connected apps on the API tokens page. Claude Code can sign in the same way. System Admins can see everyone’s connected apps, and one company switch turns off every token and connected app.

Connect your AI →

Improved

One sidebar for all your modules

Workspace, Knowledge Center, AI Hub, and Customer Experience no longer have separate menus. Every page has one sidebar listing the modules your company has turned on, in the order your System Admin sets in Admin console → Work Configuration → Modules — drag to reorder, see a live preview of what your team sees, or reset to the recommended set for your business type. Spaces now has one space picker, with My Space first and every shared space after it.

See all modules →

New

Approvals works on its own

Approvals no longer needs the Tickets module. It has its own page in the sidebar and brings Doc Approvals into the same place. Every request has a type (Document approval, Access request, Purchase request, Time off, or one your System Admin adds), each with its own stages ending approved, rejected, or withdrawn. System Admins manage types in Work Configuration → Approval Types. Approved document approvals still mark Knowledge Center pages and files as Verified. APR- approval tickets stay available for companies that use Tickets.

Read about Approvals →

Improved

AI works with your access — and personal pages stay personal

Worklynq’s AI, scheduled reports, and connected apps now all work with exactly the access of the person they act for. AI Search checks your roles before searching each source, and only searches your own Gmail if you connected it. AI never opens other people’s personal pages unless they were shared with you directly. We also fixed how shared pages work in Knowledge Center: a page you share with a few people is now visible only to them, not to the whole company.

Security & data →

Improved

End stages drawn as branches

When a ticket type can end more than one way, the workflow editor now shows each ending as a branch — Approved, Rejected, Withdrawn — marked with its outcome. You edit each ending right on its branch, and Add end stage sits underneath.

Changed

New sign-in address

Sign-up and the central sign-in page have moved to login.worklynq.net. Your workspace address stays the same.

October 22, 2026

New

QuickBooks Online

Connect QuickBooks Online and bring profit and loss, invoices, and open and overdue balances into your dashboards and reports. It’s read-only — Worklynq never changes anything in QuickBooks — and only System Admins see the numbers until one of them shares QuickBooks data with the company.

Set up QuickBooks →

New

AI builds and explains dashboards

Click Describe a dashboard, say what you want to see, and AI builds the widgets with a live preview — nothing is saved until you choose. On any dashboard or report, Explain gives you 2–5 points on what matters, what changed, and what needs attention, and you can ask follow-up questions. It uses Worklynq’s AI or a Claude or OpenAI model your company has set up.

About Dashboards & Reports →

New

Tickets can end more than one way

A ticket type can now have up to four end stages, each marked as a success, a failure, or cancelled — so a request can end Approved, Rejected, or Withdrawn. Every ticket type still needs at least one success ending. Existing ends were tagged automatically from their names.

About Tickets →

New

Dashboards for your own AI

Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor connected to Worklynq — and Worklynq’s own AI agents — can now read your dashboards and reports, with the numbers as you see them, and create or change dashboards from a conversation when you allow it.

What your AI can do →

New

Approvals module

A new, optional Workspace module for requests that need a yes or no before they go ahead — purchases, access, time off. Approval requests get their own APR- numbers and move from Requested to Under Review to Approved, Rejected, or Withdrawn. It’s off until your System Admin turns it on.

Read about Approvals →

New

Scheduled reports with an AI summary

Email any report every day, on weekdays, weekly, or monthly. Each person gets their own copy, worked out as them: a short AI summary of what the numbers say, every widget’s numbers, and a button to open the report. Anyone who can see a report can also email themselves a copy at any time.
