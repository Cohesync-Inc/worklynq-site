What’s new

New features and improvements in Worklynq, newest first.

October 27, 2026

New

Worklynq AI credits

Everything about AI use is now in Worklynq AI credits. Every person gets 1,000 credits a month, and Assist, AI Search, agents, and AI in dashboards all use it. See yours in AI Usage in your profile menu — this month’s credits, by feature and by day. System Admins see the whole company in System Admin → AI Monitoring. Assist warns you at 80%, and AI features pause at 100% until the next month or until more credits are added. Using your own Claude or OpenAI key costs no credits.

About AI credits →

Improved

A tickets list that fills the screen, with Ask AI

The tickets list now uses the whole screen, with readable columns and the Title taking the rest. A compact Ask AI box in the toolbar replaces the old query bar: type “overdue repairs at Downtown”, press Enter, and save it as a filter if you like. Filters now have two simple tabs, Saved and Fields.

Search with Ask AI →

New

Edit similar tickets together

Super admins can turn on Bulk edit, pick tickets from the same work area and of the same type, and change their status, priority, assignee, due date, sprint, or release in one go. Every field starts at Keep as is.

How bulk edit works →

New

Custom (branching) workflows

Next to Linear and Free flow, a ticket type can now use a Custom workflow: for each stage, choose which stages a ticket can move to next, so work can split into branches and loop back — Tasting → Changes requested → Tasting. A diagram shows the result, Worklynq checks every stage can be reached, and the board and status menu only allow the moves you chose.

Build a branching workflow →

Improved

Work area on New Ticket, and a Home tile for each kind of work

New Ticket now starts with one Work area field — your store, project, product, or approval space — lined up with Ticket type. Home shows a tile for each kind of work your company has open: Operations, Projects, Products, and Approval workflows, each listing its busiest work areas.

About Home →

Changed

All integrations on one page

Integrations moved into Admin console → Work Configuration → Integrations, on one page grouped by what each app is for: Sales & finance, Customers & email, Work & knowledge, Developer tools, and Custom apps. Claude, Cursor, and GitHub are set up under Developer tools. Every app uses the same tile, and the plan badges are gone.

See all integrations →

Improved

System Admin opens the console directly

System Admin in the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar now opens the Admin console straight away, on the last section you used. Move between sections inside the console.

October 26, 2026

New

Home

A new module where everyone starts: your open tickets, soonest due first; what’s overdue and due this week across the company; and how each project, store, or product is doing. It’s first in the sidebar for every company, and your System Admin can move it or turn it off.

About Home →

Improved

A new sign-up and sign-in

Signing up takes five clear steps — your email, a 6-digit code, your workspace address, your company, and your plan — with secure payment on Stripe’s checkout page. While your workspace is being built you see what’s happening, and you’re taken there when it’s ready. Sign-in, password reset, and invitations have the same new look.

Signing up → · Signing in →

Improved

First-time setup in six steps

The first time a System Admin signs in, setup walks through your company and industry, modules, your first job site, store, or product, your industry’s ticket types with their stages, the tools worth connecting, and inviting your team. Every step saves as you go, and Finish later picks up from Home.

Getting started →

New

Plans and secure checkout

Choose Basic, Advance, or Premium, monthly or yearly, priced per person. Payment happens on Stripe’s checkout page, so your card details never reach Worklynq.

Plans & billing →

Improved

Safer sign-up and password reset

Sign-up codes are now 6 digits, last 10 minutes, and stop working after 5 wrong tries; only the newest code counts. Password reset links work once. Each sign-up stays with the browser that confirmed the email, and the tools that manage every company’s workspace are limited to named Worklynq staff.

Changed

Taken to your workspace only when it’s ready

While a new workspace is still being built, signing in tells you it’s being set up instead of sending you to an address that isn’t ready yet. We email you when it is.

Signing in →

New

We can set up your workspace for you

Ask us and we’ll create your company’s workspace and email your admin a link to choose a password. The first time they sign in, setup opens as usual.

Fixed

Saving your own AI keys

Saving an AI provider’s API key in a workspace could fail. Each workspace now has its own key for storing them encrypted.

October 25, 2026

New

Custom apps

Connect your own software — a loyalty app, an ordering or booking system — without waiting for us to build a connector. Your developers add a small, read-only data feed using our one-page spec; your System Admin adds the app in Admin console → Work Configuration → Integrations → Custom apps. Every dataset becomes a source for dashboards and reports: counts, totals and averages, splits by status or tier, trends, lists, and filters like status:gold points:>100.

Connect a custom app →

New

Worklynq Assist

A chat panel on every page. Ask about your work, have it open a module or a ticket, or ask it to create and update tickets, docs, and dashboards — it asks before every change. Talk instead of typing with the microphone. Chats are saved to AI Search and easy to find again in History.

Read about Assist →

New

Shifts and who’s on duty

Staff start and end their shifts and breaks with a tap on the Operations page or by telling Assist “start my shift”. On duty now shows who’s working at each store. With Square connected, starting and ending a shift clocks people in and out on Square too.

Read about Shifts →

Improved

Order Tracking for stores

Orders can belong to a store, with one board per store and who’s on duty alongside. A Walk-in button skips looking up a customer, and the store’s Square orders appear on the board next to yours.

Order Tracking for stores →

New

TV screens

Put a store’s open orders, today’s tasks, and who’s on duty on any TV. Click Show on TV, open /tv on the TV’s browser, and type the code it shows. Screens are read-only, need no sign-in, and can be turned off at any time.

Set up a TV screen →

Coming soon

Worklynq Player

Our own small box behind the TV, managed from Operations → Screens & devices: what it shows, TV on/off schedules, restarts, updates, and screenshots, for one Player or many at once.

About the Player →

New

Order Tracking

A new Customer Experience module for following customer orders from New to Completed. Add items and prices, choose pickup, delivery, shipping, or in store, set a due time, and assign it to someone. The board has a column for each stage, overdue orders are outlined in red, and every order keeps a history of who did what. It’s on from the start for Operations and Software businesses; anyone else can turn it on in the Admin console.

Read about Order Tracking →

New

Use Worklynq from Claude and ChatGPT

Add Worklynq as a connector in Claude (claude.ai, desktop, or mobile) or ChatGPT, sign in, and click Allow — no API token needed. You choose whether the app may edit tickets, docs, and dashboards, and you can see and disconnect your connected apps on the API tokens page. Claude Code can sign in the same way. System Admins can see everyone’s connected apps, and one company switch turns off every token and connected app.

Connect your AI →

Improved

One sidebar for all your modules

Workspace, Knowledge Center, AI Hub, and Customer Experience no longer have separate menus. Every page has one sidebar listing the modules your company has turned on, in the order your System Admin sets in Admin console → Work Configuration → Modules — drag to reorder, see a live preview of what your team sees, or reset to the recommended set for your business type. Spaces now has one space picker, with My Space first and every shared space after it.

See all modules →

New

Approvals works on its own

Approvals no longer needs the Tickets module. It has its own page in the sidebar and brings Doc Approvals into the same place. Every request has a type (Document approval, Access request, Purchase request, Time off, or one your System Admin adds), each with its own stages ending approved, rejected, or withdrawn. System Admins manage types in Work Configuration → Approval Types. Approved document approvals still mark Knowledge Center pages and files as Verified. APR- approval tickets stay available for companies that use Tickets.

Read about Approvals →

Improved

AI works with your access — and personal pages stay personal

Worklynq’s AI, scheduled reports, and connected apps now all work with exactly the access of the person they act for. AI Search checks your roles before searching each source, and only searches your own Gmail if you connected it. AI never opens other people’s personal pages unless they were shared with you directly. We also fixed how shared pages work in Knowledge Center: a page you share with a few people is now visible only to them, not to the whole company.

Security & data →

Improved

End stages drawn as branches

When a ticket type can end more than one way, the workflow editor now shows each ending as a branch — Approved, Rejected, Withdrawn — marked with its outcome. You edit each ending right on its branch, and Add end stage sits underneath.

Changed

New sign-in address

Sign-up and the central sign-in page have moved to login.worklynq.net. Your workspace address stays the same.

October 22, 2026

New

QuickBooks Online

Connect QuickBooks Online and bring profit and loss, invoices, and open and overdue balances into your dashboards and reports. It’s read-only — Worklynq never changes anything in QuickBooks — and only System Admins see the numbers until one of them shares QuickBooks data with the company.

Set up QuickBooks →

New

AI builds and explains dashboards

Click Describe a dashboard, say what you want to see, and AI builds the widgets with a live preview — nothing is saved until you choose. On any dashboard or report, Explain gives you 2–5 points on what matters, what changed, and what needs attention, and you can ask follow-up questions. It uses Worklynq’s AI or a Claude or OpenAI model your company has set up.

About Dashboards & Reports →

New

Tickets can end more than one way

A ticket type can now have up to four end stages, each marked as a success, a failure, or cancelled — so a request can end Approved, Rejected, or Withdrawn. Every ticket type still needs at least one success ending. Existing ends were tagged automatically from their names.

About Tickets →

New

Dashboards for your own AI

Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor connected to Worklynq — and Worklynq’s own AI agents — can now read your dashboards and reports, with the numbers as you see them, and create or change dashboards from a conversation when you allow it.

New

Approvals module

A new, optional Workspace module for requests that need a yes or no before they go ahead — purchases, access, time off. Approval requests get their own APR- numbers and move from Requested to Under Review to Approved, Rejected, or Withdrawn. It’s off until your System Admin turns it on.

New

Scheduled reports with an AI summary

Email any report every day, on weekdays, weekly, or monthly. Each person gets their own copy, worked out as them: a short AI summary of what the numbers say, every widget’s numbers, and a button to open the report. Anyone who can see a report can also email themselves a copy at any time.

October 21, 2026

New

Modules that fit your business

Sign-up now asks What kind of company is this? — Operations, Projects, or Software — and your industry, and your workspace starts with the modules that fit. A restaurant starts with Operations, Tickets, Dashboards, Knowledge Center, and Customer Experience — and no product management. System Admins turn modules on and off in Admin console → Work Configuration → Modules: turning one on turns on what it needs, turning one off hides it without deleting anything, and pages of a module that’s off can’t be opened from an old link.

How modules work →

New

Projects module and the Operations calendar

Projects is now a module of its own, for work with a start and an end, on from the start for Projects and Software businesses. Operations gets a calendar: each day’s work by time, in a column per store or per team member, with week and month views, colors by ticket type, and overdue tasks flagged. Operations tickets share one company-wide number series, OPS-1, OPS-2, …

About Operations →

New

Customers and Engagement

Customer Experience starts with two modules. Customers keeps records for the people and businesses you sell to, with their purchases, subscriptions, orders, and total spent. Engagement adds a history of every call, meeting, email, and note, plus promo emails sent through your own Mailchimp account — pick who gets them, send now or schedule, and see opens and clicks.

About Customer Experience →

Changed

A simpler AI Hub, with agents that act

AI Hub now has two modules: AI Search and AI Agents. AI Context, Workflows, Assets, and Playground are gone — multi-step work lives in ticket stage automations, and an agent’s instructions and knowledge live in the agent itself. Agents can now look things up and, when you allow it, change tickets and docs as the person who runs them; run them yourself, on a schedule, or when a ticket reaches a stage. The Agents page also shows your team’s connected AI, the Claude and Cursor coding agents, and an activity log.
