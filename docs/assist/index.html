Worklynq Assist

Assist is a chat panel on the side of every page. Ask about your work, tell it what to open, or have it do things for you — by typing or by talking. It works as you, with your permissions, and asks before it changes anything.

Prefer to watch? This short video shows what you can ask Assist and how it gets things done. More are on Video tutorials.

Open Assist

  • On a computer, click Assist at the bottom right. On a phone, tap the Assist icon in the top bar.
  • On any computer window at least 1,024 pixels wide — a 13" laptop up to a 27" monitor — Assist sits beside the page instead of covering it, and the page rearranges itself to fit the room that’s left.
  • Assist is on every page of the workspace, including Spaces and the Knowledge Library.
  • The panel stays open while you move between pages. Press Escape to close it.

Anyone whose role can use AI Search can use Assist, when your company has AI Search turned on — including floor staff without access to AI agents.

What you can ask

  • Questions about your work: “Summarize how today is going”, “What’s still waiting at Main Street?”, “Which tickets are overdue?”
  • Open something: “Open Order Tracking”, “Show me DC-42.” Assist opens any module in your sidebar, or a ticket, and shows an Opened … chip in the chat.
  • Get things done: create, update, move or delete tickets, add comments, write or update Knowledge Center pages, and build dashboards. A deleted ticket goes to Ticket Trash, where it can be restored.
  • Your shift: “Start my shift”, “Taking a break”, “End my shift”, “When am I working next?” See Shifts.
  • Projects: “Start a project for the spring pop-up market” — Assist drafts the goals, milestones and deliverables, shows you the plan, and creates it once you say go ahead. See Projects.
  • Stock and menu: “We got 4 cases of tomatoes”, “What should I order for the weekend?”, “Which dishes should I raise prices on?” See Inventory.
  • Food safety: “Walk-in is 38”, “Did we miss any temps today?” Assist logs a reading straight away, and asks what you did about it when it’s out of range. See Food safety.
  • Training: “What training do I have?”, “Whose cards are expiring?”, “My food handler card expires March 3, 2027” — Assist saves the card once you say go ahead. See Training.
  • Turn a document into work: drop a Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF or CSV file on Assist and ask for a Spaces page, tickets, or a summary. See Work from a file.
  • Yesterday: “How did we do yesterday?” — the Daily brief in a few lines.
  • Orders: “What were our best sellers this week?”, “Who completed the most orders?” See Completed orders.

Assist answers with just the answer — it doesn’t show its working. It only states what it found in your work or on your screen, and never makes up tickets, names or numbers; if a step fails, it says so.

Assist uses the same tools as Worklynq’s AI agents — AI Search, tickets, docs, dashboards, orders, and shifts — so it can answer from everything you’re allowed to see.

Ask about what’s on your screen

Assist knows what you’re looking at, so you can ask about it without naming it — and it stays on the page you’re on while it answers:

  • A page or document: open a page in Spaces or a document in the Knowledge Library and ask “Summarize this page”, or “What do I do if the fridge is above 41°F?”
  • A ticket: with a ticket open, ask “Summarize this ticket” or “What’s it waiting on?”
  • A dashboard: on any dashboard — including a project’s or a version’s — ask “What does this dashboard show?”
  • A chat channel: “summarize this channel” or “what did we decide?” See Chat.
  • A list of tickets: on the tickets list, ask “Which of these are overdue?” Assist uses the filter you have on.

Assist reads what’s open, with your permissions, and answers beside it. Close the ticket and it goes back to the list underneath.

Work from a file

Give Assist a document and ask it to do something with it — turn the opening checklist into a Spaces page, make a ticket for each item on a punch list, or summarize a supplier’s price list.

  1. Add the file: drag it onto the Assist panel — or onto the Assist button, which opens the panel — click the paperclip, or paste it.
  2. The file shows above the message box with its size. Remove it with ×.
  3. Say what you want, or pick a suggestion: Make a Spaces page from this file, Summarize this file, or Create tickets from this file.
  • What it reads: PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx), CSV and text files, up to 15 MB each. Pictures — like a photo of an invoice — and old .doc, .xls and .ppt files aren’t read yet.
  • Pages come out formatted, with headings, lists and tables, and show as a Created · Page link in the answer. Ask for “a page per section” to split a long document.
  • The file stays with the chat, so you can keep asking about it — and it’s listed under the message you sent it with, also when you reopen the chat from History.
  • Every change asks first while a chat has files — even starting your shift or updating stock, which normally go straight through — because anyone could have written the file. Assist treats what’s in a file as information, never as instructions.
  • Questions about files use more AI credits than a short question, because Assist reads the file each time.

Changes need your OK

Looking things up happens straight away. Before Assist creates or changes a ticket, a document, or a dashboard, the panel shows Assist wants to: … with Go ahead and Don’t. You can stop it at any time while it’s working.

The one exception is starting, pausing, or ending your own shift when you ask for it — Assist does that right away, since it’s yours and easy to undo. If anything else Assist read could have prompted the change, it asks first like any other change.

Once it’s done, the answer shows a card for every ticket Assist changed — Created, Updated, Moved, Commented on or Deleted — with the ticket’s key as a link, its title and its status. A card only appears when the change really happened. Click it to open the ticket; a deleted ticket links to Ticket Trash.

Talk instead of typing

Tap the microphone and speak. Assist uses your browser’s own speech recognition (Chrome, Edge, and Safari on iPhone, iPad, and Mac), so Worklynq only receives the text. If your browser can’t do it, the microphone doesn’t show.

Handy in a kitchen or on a site: “start my shift” or “what’s next at Riverside?” without touching the screen for long.

History

  • Open History to find past chats, grouped by Today, Yesterday, Earlier this week, and then by month. Search covers titles and replies.
  • Filter by the modules your chats touched — the list follows your company’s own modules.
  • Every Assist chat is also saved in AI Search, and AI Search chats appear in Assist History with an AI Search tag so you can carry on in the panel.

Permissions and privacy

  • It acts as you. Assist sees and changes only what you can, and never other people’s personal pages.
  • Everything is logged. What Assist does shows in the agent activity log as Assist.
  • Your System Admin decides. If AI is set to read-only for the company, Assist can’t make changes — including to shifts.
  • Assist uses your company’s AI credits, like AI Search.
