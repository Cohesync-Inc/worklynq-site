Get started

People & org chart

Everyone at your company has a profile with their job title and who they report to, and the org chart puts the whole company together. This page also covers inviting people, the emails they get, and what happens when someone leaves.

Prefer to watch? This short video shows titles, managers and the org chart. More are on Video tutorials.

Your title and manager

Open your profile (your picture → Profile) and fill in Personal Info:

  • Title — your role at the company, like Shift lead or Head baker.
  • Manager — who you report to. Search by name; it never offers you or anyone who reports to you.
  • Access role shows what you can do in Worklynq — System Admin, Admin or Member. Only an admin can change it; see Roles & permissions.

Admins can set anyone’s title and manager in Admin console → User Management → Users → ⋯ → Edit user. The title shows under each name in the Users list.

The org chart

Open Profile → Org chart to see everyone nested under who they report to — every member can see it.

  • Each person shows their title and department, and how many people report to them: direct, and total at every level below.
  • Search by name, title or department. Expand all or Collapse all, or open one branch at a time.
  • No manager set lists people with no manager and nobody reporting to them yet, so you can see who’s missing.

Admins also have the org chart in User Management → Org chart, with Show deactivated, and clicking someone opens Edit user.

Someone who leaves? When a person is deleted, the people who reported to them move up to that person’s manager, so nobody drops off the chart.

Invite people

  • Invite from Admin console → User Management → Users: each person gets an email from invites@worklynq.net, with your company’s name — “Maya invited you to Harbor & Pine on Worklynq”. The link opens your own workspace, lasts 14 days, and works once.
  • Add user creates the account straight away; they get an email saying who added them, with their sign-in email and an Open Harbor & Pine button.
  • New people join as Member unless you pick another role. Only the System Admin can make someone an Admin.

Accepting an invitation is covered in Signing in.

Email notifications

Getting too much email? In Profile → Personal Info → Notifications, turn off Email notifications. It saves straight away. While it’s off, you don’t get:

  • ticket created and updated emails, and project updates;
  • your email digest and scheduled reports;
  • “added to a team” emails.

Sign-in codes, password resets and invitations always come through. Notifications in Worklynq (the bell), and desktop notifications, aren’t affected.

When someone leaves

  • Deactivate them in Edit user: they can’t sign in, and their work and history stay.
  • To delete someone, they can’t have open tickets. Worklynq lists the tickets still open, each linking to the ticket in a new tab, so you can reassign or close them and come back.
  • You can still filter tickets by people who have left: they’re listed after everyone else, marked (deactivated).
