Home

Home is where everyone starts. It shows the work that needs you, what’s overdue across the company, and how each project, store, or product is doing — so the first thing you see each morning is what to do next.

What’s on Home

  • Four counts. Open tickets, overdue, and due this week across your company, and how many open tickets are assigned to you. Click Assigned to me to open My Tickets.
  • Needs you. Your open tickets, soonest due first, with each ticket’s stage and when it’s due — Due today, Tomorrow, a weekday, or 2 days overdue. Click one to open it.
  • A tile for each kind of work. Operations, Projects, Products, and Approval workflows each get their own tile, listing the work areas with open tickets — busiest first, with their open and overdue counts. A tile only appears when that kind has open work, so you see just the mix your company uses. Each tile shows up to six; when there are more, it says Busiest 6 of N. Click one to open it.
  • Questions? Ask Assist — the button at the bottom right of every page — about your tickets, orders, and documents.
  • Finish setting up. If you’re the System Admin and chose Finish later during setup, Home has a link back to it.

A ticket counts as open until it reaches one of its type’s end stages. An RFI that’s Answered or a Submittal that’s Approved is done, even though those aren’t called “Done”.

On your phone

Home works the same on a phone, with the counts two by two and your list underneath.

Turn Home on or off

Home is a module. It’s on for every company and first in the sidebar, so it’s where Worklynq opens. Your System Admin can move it or turn it off in Admin console → Work Configuration → Modules; Worklynq then opens on your first module instead. See Modules.
