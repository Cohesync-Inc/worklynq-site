Security & data

Your business runs on what you put into Worklynq, so we treat it that way. This page explains, in plain words, how we keep your data safe and what we do — and don’t do — with it. The legal details are in our Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement.

Your own workspaceA private workspace and database for every company
EncryptedIn transit and at rest
Never used to train AIAnd never sold or used for ads
You own your dataExport it, or have it deleted

Your own workspace

Every company gets its own private Worklynq workspace at its own address (for example yourcompany.worklynq.net), with its own database and its own private file storage. Your data is kept separate from every other customer’s.

Encryption

  • Every connection to Worklynq is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Data is encrypted at rest by our cloud providers.
  • Uploaded files are kept in private storage.
  • The access you grant to connected tools — like API keys and app tokens — is stored encrypted.
  • Passwords are never stored, only secure hashes of them.

Who can see what

Your System Admin decides who joins your workspace and what each person can do. Roles and permissions control what everyone can see and change, and Worklynq’s AI follows the same rules: it only answers a person with what that person is allowed to see.

On our side, access to production systems is restricted to the people who need it to run and support Worklynq, and it’s logged. Everyone with access is bound by confidentiality.

AI and your data

  • We don’t use your content to train AI models. The AI providers we use process it only to give you results.
  • The built-in AI assistant sends only the content needed for an answer to AI models hosted on Amazon Bedrock, and gets the answer back.
  • AI coding agents (Claude, Cursor) run under your account with those providers, on the tickets you hand them and the repositories you connect. Their changes come back as pull requests your team reviews.
  • AI output can be wrong, so answers link to their sources and agents never merge their own work.

Connected tools

When you connect a tool, Worklynq can only do what you allow — for GitHub, only the repositories and permissions you grant. Connected services run under your own account with them, and your System Admin can disconnect any of them at any time.

Where your data lives

Worklynq data is stored in the United States. We use a small number of providers to run Worklynq:

ProviderWhat for
Google CloudHosting the application, its databases, and stored files
Amazon Web ServicesSending email (Amazon SES) and AI models (Amazon Bedrock)

Tools you choose to connect — like GitHub, Claude, or Cursor — work under your own account with them. We give at least 30 days’ notice before adding or replacing a provider that processes your data.

Backups, export, and deletion

  • Production databases are backed up automatically.
  • Your data belongs to your company. Your administrators can export it.
  • If you close your workspace, you have 30 days to export your content. After that we delete it from our systems within 30 days, and from backups within a further 90 days, unless the law requires us to keep something.

If something goes wrong

If there’s ever a breach affecting your data, we’ll tell you without undue delay — and within 72 hours of finding out — with what happened, what’s affected, and what we’re doing about it.

Found a security issue? Please email info@cohesync.com and we’ll look into it right away.
