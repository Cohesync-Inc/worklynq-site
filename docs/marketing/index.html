Marketing & Social

Plan your posts for Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile and LinkedIn on one calendar, with AI writing a version for each channel in your brand voice — and run campaigns with goals, dates, promo codes and tagged links.

Two modules

  • Social — the posts calendar and the post editor.
  • Marketing — campaigns, and your brand voice and settings.

Both are on for new Operations and Software companies. Others turn them on in Admin console → Work Configuration → Modules.

Worklynq doesn’t post for you yet: you copy each version to the platform and mark the post as posted. Posting straight from Worklynq, after approval, comes next.

The posts calendar

Open Social. The calendar shows the week or the month, starting on Sunday, with every post on its day and time, its channels and its status. Pick one channel to see only its posts.

  • The counts at the top: scheduled, posted, and drafts without a date.
  • Drafts without a date sit beside the calendar until you give them one.
  • Click an empty day to start a post on that day, or New post.

Write a post — with AI if you like

  1. Write the main text once and pick the channels.
  2. Each channel gets its own version, with a live count of characters and hashtags against that platform’s limit. Edit any version on its own, or Use the main text instead.
  3. Add a link, a photo idea, the campaign and location, and when it should go out. The preview shows how it looks on each channel.
  4. Write with AI (or Rewrite with AI): say what you want — “gingerbread latte is back, $5.75” — and AI writes a version for each channel in your brand voice. Nothing is posted; you check it first.

A post can be scheduled once it has a time, at least one channel, and every version within its limits. AI never makes up prices, promo codes or reviews.

Post it

  1. When it’s time, open the post and click Copy for Instagram (or another channel). It copies that version with its tagged link.
  2. Paste it on the platform and post it.
  3. Click Mark as posted and add the links to the live posts. Not posted after all undoes it.

Tagged links: links to your own website get utm_source (the channel), utm_medium and utm_campaign, so Google Analytics can show which posts bring visits. Tags already on a link are kept; links to other sites aren’t changed.

Campaigns

Open Marketing to see your campaigns — planned, live, ended or archived, from their dates.

  • New campaign: a name, goal, message or offer, start and end dates, the locations it’s for, channels, budget, promo code, and its link tag.
  • A campaign’s page shows its tracking details — the link tag, an example tagged link, the promo code — and every post in it.
  • Plan posts with AI writes 2–20 posts spread over the campaign’s dates, at most one a day. Tick the ones you want and Add as drafts.

Your brand voice

Marketing → Brand & settings is what AI reads every time it writes for you: your business name and website, what you sell and who your customers are, your tone, words to use and to avoid, how much emoji, your hashtags and sign-off, and the utm_medium for your links. The more specific it is — real products and prices — the more each post sounds like you.

Writing with AI uses your company’s AI credits.
