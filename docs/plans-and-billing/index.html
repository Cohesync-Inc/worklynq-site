Plans & billing

Worklynq is priced per person, monthly or yearly. You choose a plan and how many people when you sign up and pay on Stripe’s secure checkout page. After that, your System Admin can change plan, add people, pay invoices, and buy AI credits from Worklynq itself.

Basic

$19 per person / month
or $199 per person / year
  • Projects, operations and tickets
  • Up to 2 integrations
  • 1,000 AI credits per person a month
  • 5 GB storage
  • Support within 24 hours

Advance

$39 per person / month
or $399 per person / year
  • Everything in Basic
  • Unlimited integrations
  • 3,000 AI credits per person a month
  • Automations and workflows
  • 20 GB storage, support within 4 hours

Premium

$69 per person / month
or $699 per person / year
  • Everything in Advance
  • 7,000 AI credits per person a month
  • Unlimited storage and audit exports
  • Premium support

Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax. Every module is included in every plan — you don’t pay extra to turn one on.

How paying works

  • After you choose a plan and the number of people, Continue to secure checkout opens Stripe’s checkout page. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach Worklynq.
  • Your workspace is built only after Stripe confirms the payment. If you cancel at checkout, nothing is charged and you come back to the plan step.
  • We email you to confirm the payment. Monthly plans renew every month and yearly plans every year, until cancelled.
  • You can use a promotion code on the checkout page if you have one.

Your subscription in Worklynq

Your System Admin manages billing in Admin console → Billing (account menu → System Admin). It has three pages: Subscription, Invoices, and Credit packs.

  • Subscription shows your plan, the price per person, the AI credits each person gets, how many people you pay for against how many are active, your next payment and its date, and the card on file. If a payment is overdue, the subscription is paused or ending, or the account is suspended, a banner at the top says so.
  • Invoices lists every invoice with its amount and status. Click Pay on one that’s due, or PDF to download it. If Worklynq bills you by invoice instead of by card, the page says your invoices come by email.
  • Credit packs shows the extra AI credits left this month, lets you buy more, and lists every pack your company has bought, with Stripe receipts. See AI below.

Only your System Admin, and roles your company has given billing access, can see Billing.

Change your plan or add people

  1. Open Admin console → Billing → Subscription.
  2. Click Change plan and pick a plan, or Add users and enter how many.
  3. Worklynq shows what you’ll be charged now and your new recurring total, including tax and discounts. Check it and confirm.
ChangeWhen it appliesWhat you’re charged
A more expensive plan, or more peopleAs soon as the payment goes throughThe difference for the rest of the current period, charged to your card now
A cheaper planAt your next renewalNothing now, and nothing is refunded — the new price starts at renewal
  • If the card is declined, nothing changes and no charge is kept. If your bank asks you to confirm the payment, Worklynq shows a Confirm payment link and applies the change once you do.
  • Changed your mind about a cheaper plan? Until the renewal, Subscription shows the date it takes effect with a Keep button for your current plan. Other changes wait until you keep your plan or the switch happens.
  • Billed by invoice today? Your first change goes through Stripe’s checkout page, and from then on you pay by card.
  • A subscription that is paused, ending, overdue or suspended can’t be changed in Worklynq — contact us.

To have fewer people, switch between monthly and yearly, update your card outside an invoice, or cancel, email info@cohesync.com from your System Admin’s address.

AI

Worklynq’s built-in AI — Assist, AI Search, agents, and AI in dashboards — runs on Worklynq AI credits. Every person gets a monthly allowance with your plan: 1,000 credits on Basic, 3,000 on Advance, and 7,000 on Premium.

Need more? Your System Admin can buy extra credits for the month in Admin console → Billing → Credit packs (or with Buy credits in AI Monitoring): 1,000 for $10, 5,000 for $45, or 20,000 for $160, shared by everyone once their own credits are used. See Extra credits.

Claude and Cursor coding agents, and any feature you point at your own Claude or OpenAI key, run on your own account with those providers. They bill you for that usage directly, and it uses no Worklynq credits.
