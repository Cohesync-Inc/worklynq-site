Plans & billing

Worklynq is priced per person, monthly or yearly. You choose a plan and how many people when you sign up, and pay on Stripe’s secure checkout page.

Basic

$19 per person / month
or $199 per person / year
  • Projects, operations and tickets
  • Up to 3 integrations
  • 5 GB storage
  • Support within 24 hours

Advance

$39 per person / month
or $399 per person / year
  • Everything in Basic
  • Unlimited integrations
  • Automations and workflows
  • 20 GB storage, support within 4 hours

Premium

$69 per person / month
or $699 per person / year
  • Everything in Advance
  • Unlimited storage and audit exports
  • Premium support

Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax. Every module is included in every plan — you don’t pay extra to turn one on.

How paying works

  • After you choose a plan and the number of people, Continue to secure checkout opens Stripe’s checkout page. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach Worklynq.
  • Your workspace is built only after Stripe confirms the payment. If you cancel at checkout, nothing is charged and you come back to the plan step.
  • We email you to confirm the payment. Monthly plans renew every month and yearly plans every year, until cancelled.
  • You can use a promotion code on the checkout page if you have one.

Change your plan or number of people

To change plan, switch between monthly and yearly, add people, or cancel, email info@cohesync.com from your System Admin’s address. When you move to a bigger plan or add people mid-way, Stripe charges or credits the difference on your next invoice.

AI coding agents

Claude and Cursor coding agents run on your own account with those providers, and they bill you for that usage directly.
