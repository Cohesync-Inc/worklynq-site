Features

Inventory

Know what’s on the shelves at every location. Update stock with one tap — or by telling Assist — and let Worklynq turn your Square and Order Tracking sales into what to order and where to set your pars.

Who it’s for

Inventory is for restaurants, cafés, shops and anyone running Operations locations. It’s on for new Operations companies; others turn it on in Admin console → Work Configuration → Modules (it needs Operations). Everyone who can open Operations can count, receive and set up items.

Stock at each location

Open Inventory and pick a location. Every item is listed by category, with what’s low or out first, how many days it will last, and its par.

  • Each row has Count, + Received and Waste. They open a sheet with a big number field and +/− buttons.
  • When an item has an order unit, enter a delivery the way it arrives — “3 cases” — and Worklynq converts it (36 cartons).
  • Click an item to see its history: every count, delivery and waste, with who did it and how.
  • Add an item with the unit it’s counted in, category, on hand and par — and, if you like, supplier, cost, order unit, days from order to delivery and days between deliveries. A new location can copy its items from another one.

Count the shelves

The Count tab is made for walking the walk-in: type what’s on each shelf, press Enter to jump to the next row, and save once at the end. Rows you leave blank are skipped. Afterwards Worklynq shows where the count and your sales disagree.

Suggestions from your sales

Worklynq reads what sold — from Square at the linked location and from Order Tracking — and turns it into suggestions on the Suggestions tab. Each one shows the numbers behind it:

  • Order list — what to order before the next delivery, by supplier, in order units with cost. Copy it, and click Received when it arrives.
  • Par changes — when your sales say a par is off, with one click to apply (or apply them all).
  • Count differences — when a count is well off what sales used.
  • Stock nobody uses, menu items selling without a link, and items due a count.

Menu links

A menu link says what one sale uses — a cheeseburger is 1 bun and 0.33 lb of beef. That’s how sales become usage. The Menu links tab lists what actually sold, unlinked items first. Links are shared by every location and match stock items by name.

How the numbers work

  • On hand = the last count, plus deliveries, minus waste.
  • Expected on hand = on hand minus what sales used since that count. A count records the difference.
  • Daily usage comes from the last 28 days of sales, by weekday — or, for items no menu link uses, from your counts.
  • Suggested par = what you use between ordering and the next delivery after that, plus 20%.

Just tell Assist

Say it or type it to Assist:

  • “We got 4 cases of tomatoes.” “Count 12 lb of ground beef.” “Tossed 3 lb of fries.” — Assist books it straight away.
  • “What should I order for the weekend?” “Are my pars right?” “Why is cheddar short?”
  • “Link my unlinked menu items.” — Assist drafts the amounts and saves them once you approve.
