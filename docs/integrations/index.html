How connections work

  • Only your System Admin can connect or disconnect tools.
  • You choose the access. Worklynq can only reach what you allow — for GitHub, the repositories and permissions you grant.
  • Your account, your bill. Connected services run under your own account with them. For AI agents, usage is billed to you by Anthropic or Cursor.
  • Read-only where it should be. Business systems are connected read-only — Worklynq never takes payments or changes your books. The one exception: with Square, Worklynq clocks people in and out when they start and end their own shift.
  • Stored securely. Keys and tokens you give Worklynq are stored encrypted. See Security & data.
  • Disconnect anytime from the Admin console. Apps you connected yourself, like Claude or ChatGPT, are disconnected from your profile menu → API tokens.
