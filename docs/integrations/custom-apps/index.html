Integrations

Custom apps

Bring data from your own software into Worklynq — a loyalty app, an ordering or booking system, anything built for your business. Your app’s developers add a small, key-protected data feed, your System Admin adds the app in Worklynq, and its data shows up in your dashboards and reports. No waiting for us to build a connector.

What it does

  • Every dataset your app shares — customers, visits, bookings, rewards — becomes a source for Dashboards & Reports.
  • Count records, total or average any number, split by categories like status or tier, show trends over time, or list records — for example, “Gold members with more than 100 points”.
  • Works with everything else in dashboards: Describe a dashboard, Explain, and scheduled reports.
  • Read-only. Worklynq only reads from the feed and never changes anything in your app.

Before you start

  • Your app needs a Worklynq data feed. Send your developers the feed spec — it’s one page. They’ll give you the feed’s address and a key.
  • You need to be your workspace’s System Admin.

Add your app

  1. Open Admin console → Connectors → Custom apps and click Add custom app.
  2. Enter the app’s name, the feed address (it starts with https://), and the key from your developers.
  3. Click Test feed. Worklynq lists the datasets it found.
  4. Click Add app.

Use it in dashboards

In Dashboards or Reports, add a widget and pick App name: Dataset under Data from. Then choose:

  • What to show: the number of records, or the total or average of a number field (points, visits, spend).
  • How to split it: by a category field, in the order your app lists its values — Bronze, Silver, Gold.
  • When: any range from today to the last 12 months, or All time — the default, so balances and current totals include everyone.
  • How to show it: a number, bars, a table, a list, or a trend over time when the dataset has a date.
  • Filter: for example status:gold points:>100 or -status:bronze. Every part must match.

Or just describe it: “a dashboard of loyalty members by status, with points totals” — AI builds it from your app’s real fields.

Who can see the data

By default, only System Admins can use a custom app’s data. To share a dataset, a System Admin opens Dashboards → Connected data and changes who can use it. Widgets always run as the person looking, so people without access see a note instead of the numbers.

Manage your app

  • Preview the first records of a dataset to check what Worklynq sees.
  • Refresh datasets after your app adds new data or fields.
  • Change the feed address or key — Worklynq tests the new one before saving.
  • Pause an app to stop reading from it, or remove it. Adding the same feed address again brings its widgets back.
  • Changes in your app show up within 5 minutes.

Security

  • The key is stored encrypted and never shown again — Worklynq shows only its last four characters.
  • Feeds must use https on a public address. Worklynq won’t connect to private networks and doesn’t follow redirects.
  • Only System Admins can add, change, preview, or remove apps.
  • Share only what your dashboards need — the feed spec asks developers to leave out emails, phone numbers, and other personal details unless you need them.

Troubleshooting

The app shows “Needs attention”
The feed stopped answering or rejected the key. The message shows your app’s own error — pass it to your developers.
Test feed fails
Check the address starts with https://, has nothing after a ?, and is reachable from the internet; then check the key.
A new field or dataset is missing
Click Refresh datasets.
Numbers look out of date
Worklynq keeps answers for up to 5 minutes. If they’re older than that, check whether the app is paused.
