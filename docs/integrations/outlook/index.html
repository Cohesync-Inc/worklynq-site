Integrations

Outlook

Connect one company mailbox on Microsoft 365 or Outlook.com — bookings@, catering@, hello@ — and see how much email comes in, and who it’s from, next to the work it turns into. Worklynq only reads: it never sends, moves or deletes email.

Prefer to watch? This short video shows how to connect Outlook and where its data shows up. More are on Video tutorials.

What it does

  • Connects one Outlook mailbox for the whole company, once.
  • Adds an Outlook (company mailbox) widget to dashboards and reports: emails today, per day, or this month, and who emails you most.

New in the beta: this connection is being switched on workspace by workspace. If you can’t connect yet, or you see “isn’t set up on this Worklynq server yet”, email info@cohesync.com and we’ll turn it on for you.

What Worklynq reads

  • The mailbox’s address and name
  • When each email in the Inbox arrived, and who sent it

All access is read-only. Worklynq never sends, moves or deletes email, and only one company mailbox is connected — nobody’s personal inbox.

Before you start

  • You need to be your workspace’s System Admin.
  • You need the sign-in for the mailbox you want to connect. Some Microsoft 365 companies only let their IT admin approve new apps — if Microsoft asks for an admin’s approval, send them the request it shows.

Connect Outlook

  1. Open Admin console → Integrations and choose Outlook under Customers & email.
  2. Click Continue to Microsoft and sign in with the mailbox you want to connect.
  3. Review what Worklynq will read and click Accept.
  4. You’re back in Worklynq, and Outlook shows Connected with the mailbox’s address.

Outlook in dashboards

In Dashboards or Reports, click Add widget and ask for it — “emails to bookings this week” — or choose Outlook (company mailbox) as the data.

  • Splitting by sender looks at the 300 most recent emails in the range.

Who can see the numbers

To start, only System Admins can use Outlook widgets. To share them, a System Admin opens Dashboards → Connected data and sets Outlook to everyone in the company. On a shared dashboard, people who aren’t allowed see a note instead of the numbers.

Change the mailbox or disconnect

  • Use a different mailbox signs in again with another one.
  • Disconnect deletes Worklynq’s saved sign-in, and Outlook widgets stop showing data. To remove Worklynq from the Microsoft account too, open myapps.microsoft.com and remove it there.

Troubleshooting

“Needs reconnecting”
Microsoft stopped accepting Worklynq’s sign-in — for example, the password changed or access was removed. Open Outlook in Integrations and click Reconnect Outlook.
Microsoft says an admin has to approve the app
Your company’s Microsoft 365 only lets its IT admin approve apps. Ask them to approve Worklynq, then connect again.
The numbers look low
Only the Inbox is counted. Email that rules move into other folders isn’t included.
