Integrations

Square

Connect your Square point of sale so Worklynq can show sales, orders, and refunds for each of your stores on your dashboards and reports. Worklynq only reads from Square — it never takes payments or changes anything in your Square account.

What it does

  • Connects your company’s Square seller account to Worklynq, once, for the whole company.
  • Links each Square location to the matching store in Worklynq, so sales line up with the work happening there.
  • Adds a Square sales widget to Dashboards and Reports: sales, orders, average order, refunds, and items sold — by store, item, payment type, day, or hour.

What Worklynq reads

From SquareWhy
Your business profile and locationsTo link Square locations to your stores
Orders, payments, and refundsSales, orders, and refunds on your dashboards
Items, inventory, and customersItems sold today; more to come
Team members and timecardsStaffing by store, as it’s added

All access is read-only. Worklynq can’t take payments, issue refunds, change prices or items, or edit anything in Square.

Before you start

  • You need to be your workspace’s System Admin.
  • You need to sign in to the Square account that owns your locations, with permission to approve apps for it.
  • Add your stores in Worklynq first (see Set up how you organize work), so you can link each Square location to one.

Connect Square

  1. In Worklynq, open the Admin console → Connectors → Integrations and choose Square.
  2. Review what Worklynq will read, then click Continue to Square.
  3. Sign in to Square and approve access.
  4. You’re taken back to Worklynq. Square shows as Connected with your Square account and its locations.

Link your locations

Right after connecting, you’ll see your Square locations. For each one, choose the Worklynq store it belongs to, then click Save mapping.

  • A location you don’t link shows as Not linked. A dashboard for a store only shows sales from the Square locations linked to it.
  • Locations that are inactive in Square are marked Inactive.

Name your Worklynq stores the way your team knows them — “Downtown café”, “Riverside” — so the mapping is obvious.

Check the connection

Under Last 7 days, click Show sales. You’ll see completed orders, sales, and refunds for each location and for all locations together, straight from Square. If the numbers look right, you’re set.

Square in dashboards

In Dashboards or Reports, add a Square sales widget and choose:

  • What to show: sales, orders, average order, refunds, or items sold.
  • How to split it: by location, item, payment type, day of the week, or hour of the day.
  • How to show it: a number, bars, a trend over time, or a table.
  • When: any range from today to the last 12 months.

Pin a dashboard to one store, and its Square widgets show only that store’s linked locations — a dashboard per store, with sales right next to its checklists and requests.

Numbers come from completed Square orders and can be up to 5 minutes behind. Days and hours follow each Square location’s own timezone. If your locations use more than one currency, only your main currency is counted, and the widget says so.

Who can see sales

By default, only System Admins can use Square widgets. To share them, a System Admin opens Dashboards → Connected data and sets Square to everyone in the company. Widgets always run as the person looking, so on a shared dashboard, people who aren’t allowed to see Square data see a note instead of the numbers.

Keep it up to date

  • Opened a new location in Square? Click Refresh from Square, then link it to its store.
  • You don’t need to reconnect. Worklynq keeps the connection fresh on its own, even if it isn’t used for a while.

Disconnect

Open Square in Admin console → Connectors → Integrations and click Disconnect. Worklynq stops reading from Square, its access is revoked at Square, and Square widgets stop showing data. You can also remove Worklynq in your Square account under Apps.

Troubleshooting

Square shows “Needs attention”
Worklynq’s access was removed or stopped working — for example, someone removed Worklynq under Apps in Square. Disconnect and connect again.
“No locations found in this Square account”
You signed in to a Square account without locations. Disconnect, then connect again with the account that owns your stores.
A store’s dashboard shows no Square sales
Link that store to its Square location under Link your locations.
“Square isn’t set up on this Worklynq server yet”
Contact us at info@cohesync.com and we’ll turn it on for your workspace.
A new location is missing
Click Refresh from Square.
