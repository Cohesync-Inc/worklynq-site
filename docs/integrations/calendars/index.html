Integrations

Google Calendar & Microsoft Calendar

Connect your company’s Google or Microsoft calendars — catering, private dining, interviews, site visits — and put what’s booked next to the work. See what’s coming up, how many meetings and hours are booked, and when you’re busiest. Worklynq only reads your calendars.

Prefer to watch? This short video shows how to connect your calendars and use them on dashboards. More are on Video tutorials.

What it does

  • Connects a Google or Microsoft account for the whole company, and lets you choose which of its calendars count — up to 20.
  • Adds a Calendar events widget to dashboards and reports. It combines both providers, so you can connect one or both.

New in the beta: this connection is being switched on workspace by workspace. If you can’t connect yet, or you see “isn’t set up on this Worklynq server yet”, email info@cohesync.com and we’ll turn it on for you.

What Worklynq reads

  • The calendars the account can see, and their names
  • For the calendars you choose: each event’s title, start and end, organizer, and whether it’s all day or marked free

All access is read-only. Worklynq never creates, changes or deletes events.

Connect a calendar

  1. As the System Admin, open Admin console → Integrations and choose Google Calendar or Microsoft Calendar under Calendars.
  2. Sign in with the account whose calendars you want, and approve read-only access.
  3. Back in Worklynq, choose the Calendars that count. The account’s main calendar is already ticked; add shared ones like Catering & events or Interviews, and leave out ones like public holidays.
  4. Click Done. Change the choice any time; Reload list picks up calendars added since.

Calendars in dashboards

Click Add widget and ask — “meetings this week by calendar”, “what’s coming up” — or choose Calendar events as the data.

  • Ready-made: Coming up, Meetings this week, Hours booked per week, and Busiest hours.
  • Narrow it down with Title contains — for example, only events with “interview” in the title.
  • A list for today or the next 7 days shows what’s still to come, soonest first.
  • A meeting on more than one chosen calendar — or on both Google and Microsoft — counts once.

Who can see the events

To start, only System Admins can use calendar widgets. A System Admin can open them to everyone in Dashboards → Connected data. On a shared dashboard, people who aren’t allowed see a note instead.

Good to know

  • Calendar events show on dashboards and reports. They don’t appear on the Operations calendar or in AI Search yet.
  • One account per provider is connected for the company, not each person’s own calendar.
  • Use a different account switches accounts; Disconnect removes Worklynq’s saved sign-in.
