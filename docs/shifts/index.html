Features

Shifts

See who’s on duty, plan the week’s shifts, and see how they went. People start and end their own shifts — with a tap, or by telling Assist — and if you use Square, Worklynq clocks them in and out there too.

Find your shifts

Open Operations and click the Shifts tab (next to Calendar), or follow My shifts → from the On duty strip. Shifts are part of the Operations module: each shift is one person at one location.

Shifts opens on My shifts for everyone:

  • whether you’re on duty right now, how many shifts are coming up, and hours planned and worked this week;
  • Coming up — your planned shifts for the next 30 days, at every location, with your next shift first;
  • Past shifts — what you worked in the last 30 days, with clock-in and out, breaks and any lateness.

The team schedule

Team schedule shows the week, Sunday to Saturday, with a row for each person:

  • Each planned shift shows its time and role, colored by how it went: upcoming, on duty, worked, late (clocked in more than 10 minutes after the start), not in yet, or missed. A shift someone worked without one planned shows dashed.
  • The right column compares planned and worked hours for each person; the bottom row shows how many people and hours each day has.
  • Use the arrows to move week by week, and pick one location or all of them.

Upcoming lists the next 14 days of planned shifts by day. Completed lists finished shifts for a period — this week, last week, the last 14 or 30 days — with clock-in and out, breaks, worked time and the planned shift it matched, plus totals and a CSV export.

Plan shifts

Managers — System Admins, and roles that manage tickets or projects — plan the schedule. Everyone else sees it read-only.

  • + Add shift, or click a day in the team schedule: pick the person, location, start and end, and a role or note.
  • Click a shift to change or remove it.
  • Copy last week copies every shift into this week, skipping any that would overlap one already planned.
  • One person can’t have two shifts that overlap, and a shift is at most 16 hours.

Start, pause and end a shift

The On duty now strip on Operations — and, with a store chosen, on the Order Tracking board — has buttons for you: Start my shift, Take a break / End break, and End my shift. It also says when your next shift is.

  • With Assist: say or type “start my shift”, “taking a break” or “end my shift”. Assist does it straight away, since it’s your own shift. Ask “who’s on at Riverside?” or “when am I working next?” too.
  • Which store: the one you name; otherwise your only one; otherwise the one you used last; otherwise Worklynq asks.
  • One open shift per person. Ending a shift ends a break that’s still running. A shift open for more than 16 hours is marked over 16 h — usually someone forgot to clock out.
  • Put who’s on duty on a TV in the store with TV screens.

Clock in on Square

If your company uses Square, starting a shift also clocks the person in on Square, and ending it clocks them out. That happens when:

  • Square is connected and the store is linked to its Square location, and
  • the person is an active Square team member with the same email as their Worklynq sign-in.

If any of that is missing, the Worklynq shift still starts — the shift notes why it didn’t reach Square, so you can clock in on Square by hand. Breaks are kept in Worklynq.

If Square was connected before clock-ins were added, a System Admin needs to reconnect Square once to allow them.

Control

  • If your System Admin sets AI to read-only, Assist can’t start or end shifts; the buttons still work.
  • Apps connected to Worklynq can only change shifts with the Start and end your shifts permission. See Connect your AI.
