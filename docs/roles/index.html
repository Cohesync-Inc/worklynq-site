Roles & permissions

A role decides what someone can do in each module: No access, View, Use or Manage. Everyone starts as a Member; your System Admin and Admins can change what Members can do, and make roles of their own.

The three built-in roles

System Admin
Can do everything, always. The only one who changes the plan, buys AI credits, turns modules on or off, sets up company-wide integrations and locations, and makes someone an Admin or System Admin. The person who signs your company up is its System Admin.
Admin
Runs the company day to day: every module at its highest level, plus people, teams and roles. Admins can see billing but not change it, and can’t change what Admins themselves can do — only the System Admin can.
Member
Everyone else, and anyone who hasn’t been given a role. Members use the everyday modules — tickets, operations, inventory counts, food safety checks, their own training, chat, docs, AI Search — but don’t see the Admin console.

Your company can add its own roles, such as Shift lead or Barista. See Make your own role.

Access levels

  • No access — the module is hidden from them.
  • View — they can look but not change anything.
  • Use — they do the everyday work: create, edit, record and reply.
  • Manage — they also set the module up and act on other people’s work, like planning everyone’s shifts or signing off training.

Each level includes the ones before it. Not every module has all four: some only make sense as View or Manage. Someone with several roles gets the highest level any of them gives.

Modules your company has turned off don’t appear on the Roles page or in anyone’s sidebar. Turning a module back on uses the levels you set before.

Workspace

  • Tickets — View: See tickets, comments and history. Use: Create, edit, comment on, move and assign tickets; delete their own. Manage: Also delete and restore anyone's tickets and empty the trash.
  • Team Board — View: See boards, backlogs and sprints. Use: Create, start, finish and change sprints.
  • Roadmaps — View: See roadmaps. Use: Create and change roadmaps.
  • Projects — View: See public projects and their plans. Use: Start projects and run the ones they own (private projects stay with their owners).
  • Operations (calendar, shifts, daily brief) — View: See the calendar, the daily brief and who's on shift. Use: Schedule tasks and start, pause and end their own shifts. Manage: Also plan and publish everyone's shifts.
  • Inventory — View: See stock levels and order lists. Use: Count stock and record deliveries and waste. Manage: Also add, edit and archive items, copy a location's items and set up menu costs.
  • Food safety — View: See food safety checks and logs. Use: Record temperatures and complete checks. Manage: Also set up checks, equipment and limits.
  • Training — View: See their own training and certifications. Use: Do their training and add their own certification cards. Manage: Also build courses, assign training, sign off steps and verify cards.
  • Approvals — View: See approval requests. Use: Ask for approvals and approve what's sent to them.
  • Chat — View: Read channels. Use: Post, react, start channels and direct messages. Manage: Also rename or archive any channel and delete anyone's messages.
  • Dashboards — View: See dashboards shared with them. Use: Build their own dashboards and duplicate others'. Manage: Also change or delete anyone's dashboards.
  • Scheduled reports — View: See scheduled reports. Use: Schedule reports and email themselves a copy.
  • TV screens and Players — View: See the company's screens and Players. Manage: Set up, pair and change screens and Players.
  • Products and releases — View: See products, versions and release dashboards. Use: Create and change versions and releases. Manage: Also delete versions and change products.

AI Hub

  • AI Search and Assist — View: Ask AI Search and Assist (uses AI credits). Use: Also create AI projects, context, workflows and assets. Manage: Also turn AI models on or off.
  • AI Agents — View: See agents and their runs. Use: Run agents (uses AI credits). Manage: Also create, change and delete agents.

Knowledge Center

  • Spaces and Knowledge Library — View: Read pages in the spaces they belong to. Use: Write and edit pages (each space still decides who's in it). Manage: Also manage the Knowledge Center's files.

Customer Experience

  • Customers — View: See customers. Use: Add and edit customers and their orders. Manage: Also delete customers.
  • Order Tracking — View: See orders and their stages. Use: Add orders, move them through stages and add notes.
  • Engagement — View: See email templates and campaigns. Use: Write templates and send campaigns to customers. Manage: Also connect or disconnect the email marketing account.
  • Social — View: See posts, comments and reviews. Use: Write and schedule posts and reply to comments and reviews.
  • Marketing — View: See marketing plans and campaigns. Use: Plan and change campaigns. Manage: Also change marketing settings.

Admin console

  • People and teams — View: See everyone in the company and their teams (needed to assign work). Use: Also invite people and change teams. Manage: Also edit, suspend and remove people. Only the System Admin and Admins change roles.
  • Work configuration — View: See ticket types, fields and workflows (needed to create tickets). Manage: Change ticket types, fields, screens and workflows.
  • Integrations — View: See which tools are connected and use their data. Use: Also connect and disconnect their own accounts. Only the System Admin sets up company-wide connections.
  • Billing — View: See the subscription, invoices and credit packs. Only the System Admin changes the plan or buys credits.
  • Admin tools — View: See AI Monitoring, usage and the admin activity log. Manage: Also export data, set up webhooks and change company email settings.

Change what a role can do

  1. Open Admin console → User Management → Roles.
  2. Pick the role on the left.
  3. Choose a level for each module. The note next to it says what that level allows.
  4. Click Save changes. People with the role get the new access within a minute.

Only the System Admin and Admins can change roles. Only the System Admin can change the Admin role, and nobody can change the System Admin.

Make your own role

  1. In Admin console → User Management → Roles, click New role.
  2. Give it a name and a short description.
  3. Start from What Members can do or from No access, then set each module.

Rename or delete it from the same page. When you delete a role, the people who had it become Members.

Give someone a role

Choose the role when you invite them, or later in Admin console → User Management → Users. Only the System Admin can make someone an Admin or System Admin. Your company always keeps at least one System Admin.

Questions

Does AI see more than I can?
No. AI Search and Assist only read what your role lets you see, and any change Assist makes is made as you.
Someone can’t see a module.
Check that the module is on (Modules) and that their role isn’t set to No access for it. If they were just given access, have them reload the page.
Spaces, projects and direct messages
A role lets someone into a module; some things inside it have their own members. Each space decides who’s in it, private projects stay with their owners, and a direct message is only for the two people in it.
