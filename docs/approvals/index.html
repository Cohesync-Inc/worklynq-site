Approvals

Some things need a yes or no before they go ahead — a purchase, access to a system, time off, a change to a menu or a price. The Approvals module gives those requests their own place, their own numbers, and a clear path from asked to answered.

Optional. Approvals is off for every company until your System Admin turns it on — whatever your business type.

What it’s for

  • Purchases — new equipment, a supplier change, anything over a spending limit.
  • Access — a new account, a key, permissions to a system.
  • Time off and shift swaps.
  • Anything else where someone has to sign off before work starts.

Approvals sit alongside your products, projects, and stores as a fourth kind of work, so requests never get lost in the general task list.

Turn on Approvals

  1. As System Admin, open the Admin console → Modules.
  2. Switch on Approvals (it needs the Tickets module) and save. The change applies to everyone in your company.
  3. Worklynq sets you up automatically: an Approvals space, with the ready-made Approval Request ticket type mapped to it.

Turning Approvals off later only hides it — your requests and their history stay.

Ask for approval

  1. Create a ticket. Approvals appears as its own group, next to products, projects, and operations.
  2. Choose the Approvals space and the Approval Request type — only ticket types mapped to that space are offered.
  3. Say what you need and why, add any quote or document, and assign it to the person who decides.

How a request moves

Requested→Under Review→ ApprovedRejectedWithdrawn
StageWhat it means
RequestedSomeone has asked. Nobody has looked yet.
Under ReviewThe approver is looking at it.
ApprovedYes — the work can go ahead.
RejectedNo. Add a comment so the requester knows why.
WithdrawnThe requester no longer needs it.

A request can end in any of the three final stages, and Worklynq knows which ones mean yes, no, and cancelled.

APR numbers

Every approval request gets a number from one company-wide sequence — APR-1, APR-2, and so on — just like operations tickets use OPS. Mention “APR-14” in a comment or a chat and everyone knows exactly which request you mean.

APR and OPS are reserved, so a product or project name never ends up with those letters.

Ticket types for approvals

The Approval Request type comes ready to use. You can rename it, change its stages, or add your own approval types — say, Purchase Request with an extra stage for a second approver — in Admin console → Work Configuration → Ticket types, then map them to your Approvals space in Ticket Type Mapping, where approval spaces are listed after operations.

The ready-made Approval Request type only shows in the ticket type library while the Approvals module is on.

Tips

  • Ask your team to put the cost or the dates in the title — “New espresso grinder, $1,200” — so approvers can decide at a glance.
  • Build a dashboard of open approval requests so nothing waits too long.
  • Write your approval rules (who approves what, and spending limits) in Knowledge Center, so AI Search can answer “Who approves purchases over $500?”
