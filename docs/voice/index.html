Features

Voice

Ask Assist out loud and hear the answer, in the same voice as our videos. Handy when your hands are full: on the line, on a job site, or walking the floor.

Prefer to watch? This one-minute video shows a question, a spoken answer, and a ticket approved by voice. More are on Video tutorials.

Ask with the mic

  1. Open Assist: the button at the bottom right, or the Assist icon in the top bar on a phone.
  2. Tap the microphone. The first time, your browser asks to use it: choose Allow.
  3. Ask, the way you’d ask a colleague: “What’s still waiting at Downtown?” or “Log a ticket for the walk-in fridge.”
  4. Stop talking. Assist notices the pause and sends your question. You can also tap the mic again when you’re done.

Your words show in the chat, like a typed question. It works in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox, on computers, phones and tablets. A question can be up to 30 seconds long.

Hear the answer

When you ask by voice, Assist starts its answer with a sentence or three and reads them aloud. The details, such as lists, tables and links, stay on screen to look at when you’re ready.

  • Stop next to Speaking… cuts it short. Tapping the mic also stops it, so you can ask the next thing straight away.
  • Questions you type are never read aloud.

Say “go ahead”

Before Assist changes anything, it tells you what it will do and asks. When you asked by voice, it reads that out and listens for your answer:

  • “Go ahead”, “yes” or “do it”: Assist makes the change.
  • “Don’t”, “no” or “cancel”: nothing changes.
  • Anything else: it shows what it heard and waits for you to tap Go ahead or Don’t.

The same things need your OK as when you type. See Changes need your OK.

Turn it on or off

  • In Assist: the speaker button at the top of the panel turns spoken answers off, or back on.
  • In your profile: Voice has Speak Assist’s answers and Speaking speed: Slower, Normal, Faster or Fastest. Both are saved in the browser you’re using.
  • For the whole company: a System Admin can turn Voice for Assist off in Admin console → AI Monitoring. It’s on to start with. With it off, Assist doesn’t speak, recordings aren’t sent to Worklynq, and the mic uses the browser’s own speech recognition where there is one.

Privacy and AI credits

  • Nothing is recorded until you tap the mic, and the red dot shows while Assist is listening.
  • Your recording goes to Worklynq only to be turned into text, on Worklynq’s own servers, and is then thrown away. Only the text is kept, in the chat, like a typed question.
  • Spoken answers are made from the answer’s text and aren’t kept either.
  • Asking by voice uses the same AI credits as typing. Listening and speaking use none.

“Hey Worklynq” Coming soon

Next, you’ll be able to say “Hey Worklynq” to start a question without tapping anything. It will be a setting you turn on yourself in Profile → Voice, it will listen only while Worklynq is open on your screen, and it will notice the phrase on your own device, so nothing is sent until you say it.
