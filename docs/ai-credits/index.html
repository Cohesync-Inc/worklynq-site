Worklynq AI credits

Worklynq’s built-in AI — Assist, AI Search, AI agents, and AI in dashboards — runs on Worklynq AI credits. Every person gets 1,000 credits a month, you can see exactly where they go, and Worklynq tells you before they run out.

What uses credits

FeatureUses credits when…
Assistyou send it a message
AI Searchyou ask a question
AI agentsa Worklynq agent runs — by hand, on a schedule, or from a ticket stage
Dashboards & ReportsAI builds or explains a dashboard, or writes a scheduled report’s summary

Bigger requests use more credits than small ones. Requests that fail without an answer use none.

No credits for your own AI. When your company uses its own Claude or OpenAI API key for a feature, that use costs no Worklynq credits — your provider bills you directly. The same goes for Claude and Cursor coding agents, and for your own Claude or ChatGPT connected to Worklynq.

Monthly allowance

  • 1,000 credits per person per month, included for everyone on your team. Each person’s allowance is their own — one busy person can’t use up someone else’s.
  • As a rough guide, a typical Assist question uses about 5 credits, so 1,000 credits is around 200 questions a month.
  • Worklynq can set a different allowance for one person, or a limit for the whole company, if you ask.
  • Allowances start again at the beginning of each calendar month. Unused credits don’t carry over.
  • Need more? Your System Admin can ask Worklynq at info@cohesync.com to raise a person’s or the company’s limit, or add extra credits.

Before you run out

  • At 80%, Assist shows how much you’ve used under the message box — for example, You’ve used 82% of your Worklynq AI credits this month.
  • At 100%, Assist, AI Search, agents, and AI in dashboards stop and say You’ve used this month’s Worklynq AI credits. Ask your administrator for more credits. If it’s the company limit that’s reached, the message says so.
  • Everything else in Worklynq keeps working — only the AI features pause until the next month or until more credits are added.

See your own usage

Open your profile menu and choose AI Usage. You’ll see this month’s credits against your allowance, credits by feature, credits per day, and your recent AI activity.

See your company’s usage

System Admins open System Admin → AI Monitoring to see the company’s credits this month against its limit, each person’s credits and allowance, and credits by feature. It’s read-only — to change limits, contact Worklynq.

Turning AI off

AI can be turned off for a whole company or for one person. Ask Worklynq at info@cohesync.com. When it’s off, AI features say so and the rest of Worklynq works as usual.
