Sales & finance

See the money next to the work that makes it.

Connect your point of sale and your books once, for the whole company. Worklynq reads the numbers and puts them beside the checklists, orders and projects behind them.

S

Square

Available now

Every store’s sales, orders and shifts, lined up with the work happening there.

Connect your Square seller account once and link each Square location to its store in Worklynq. From then on, Square is part of how every store runs its day.

  • Sales on every store’s dashboard. Sales, orders, average order, refunds and items sold, split by store, item, payment type, day or hour.
  • Square orders on the board and the TV. Each store’s open Square orders show on its Order Tracking board and kitchen TV, right next to your Worklynq orders.
  • Clock in by starting a shift. When someone starts or ends their shift in Worklynq, they’re clocked in or out on Square too.
  • A dashboard per store. Pin a dashboard to one store and its Square numbers show only that store’s locations, beside its checklists and requests.
  • In your emailed reports. Add Square widgets to the reports Worklynq emails out on a schedule.

Read-only, apart from timecards. Worklynq never takes payments, issues refunds, or changes prices, items or orders in Square. Numbers are at most 5 minutes behind.

Q

QuickBooks

Available now

Profit, invoices and open balances, right where you make decisions.

Connect QuickBooks Online once for the company, then put its numbers on any dashboard or scheduled report, next to the store, job or project they belong to.

  • Profit and loss at a glance. Income, cost of sales, gross profit, expenses and net income, as a total or as a trend by day, week or month.
  • Invoices that need chasing. Amount invoiced, open and overdue balances, and what’s due in the next 7 days, by customer or by status.
  • Numbers that match your books. Worklynq follows your cash or accrual setting and QuickBooks’ own weeks, and every invoice links straight to QuickBooks.
  • You decide who sees them. QuickBooks numbers start out for System Admins only. Share them when you’re ready; anyone without access sees a note instead.

Read-only. Worklynq can’t create invoices, record payments or change anything in QuickBooks. Works with QuickBooks Online.

Marketing & customers

Post, reply and measure from where you run the business.

Plan posts for every channel on one calendar, answer comments and reviews in one inbox, and see what brings people in. AI writes in your brand voice, and you check everything before it goes out.

G

Google

Rolling out in the beta

Know how people find you, and what to do about it.

One Google connection brings in Search Console, Google Analytics and your Business Profile locations, each linked to its store. It all shows up in Marketing → SEO & website.

  • What people search to find you. Clicks, times shown, click-through rate and position, with your top searches and pages, against the period before.
  • Ideas from real searches. “Almost on page 1” finds searches where you sit just off the first page, and AI suggests posts and pages that answer them.
  • Better titles for pages people skip. For pages that show up but rarely get clicked, AI writes new titles and descriptions from that page’s own searches.
  • Your Business Profile, store by store. Views on Search and Maps, calls, directions and website clicks, a profile checklist, local posts and review replies.
  • Which posts bring visits. Tagged links show the visits each post and campaign sends to your website.

Read-only, apart from Business Profile posts and review replies. Worklynq never changes your website or your profile details.

f

Facebook + Instagram

Rolling out in the beta

Plan the week’s posts once. Worklynq posts them on time.

Connect your Facebook Pages and the Instagram account on each, and choose which store every Page belongs to.

  • One post, the right version for each channel. Write it once, or have AI write it in your brand voice. Each channel gets its own version, checked against its limits.
  • Posted when you said. Scheduled posts go out on their own, in your time zone. If a platform refuses one, Worklynq tries again and tells you why.
  • Approvals when you want them. Turn approvals on and nothing is posted until the right person says yes.
  • See how every post did. Views, reactions, comments and shares for each channel and each post, over 7, 30 or 90 days.
  • Comments in one inbox. Answer with AI reply suggestions, or turn a complaint into a ticket in one click.

Read-only, apart from posts and replies. Instagram accounts need to be professional accounts linked to a Facebook Page. Until a channel is connected, you can still plan posts, copy them and mark them as posted.

in

LinkedIn

Rolling out in the beta

Show up where clients and future hires look.

Post to the LinkedIn Company Pages you manage, on the same calendar as every other channel.

  • Scheduled company posts. Links turn into article cards, and photos upload with the post.
  • Results next to your other channels. See how each post did, side by side with Facebook, Instagram and Google.
  • Comments in the Social Inbox. Reply as your Page, with AI suggesting replies in your brand voice.
  • A heads-up before it lapses. LinkedIn sign-ins last 60 days. Worklynq warns you a week before, so scheduled posts keep going out.

Read-only, apart from posts and replies. You need to be an admin of the Company Page.

M

Mailchimp

Available now

Promo emails to the customers who’ll actually care.

With the Engagement module, write a promo email, pick who gets it from what and when they bought, and send it through your own Mailchimp account.

  • Audiences from real purchases. Pick by status, tags and recent purchases, like “active customers who bought in the last 90 days”.
  • Send now or schedule. Send yourself a test first, and save the emails you send often as templates.
  • See what worked. Opens, clicks and unsubscribes for every campaign.
  • Only people who said yes. Promo emails only go to customers who agreed to receive them.

Your Mailchimp account, your sending domain. Worklynq picks the audience and writes the email; Mailchimp delivers it.

G

GitHub, Claude & Cursor

Available now

Hand the ticket to an AI agent. Get a pull request back.

Connect GitHub, then Claude, Cursor or both. Agents pick up bugs, stories and small features from your tickets, work in the right repository, and your team reviews what comes back.

GGitHub

Gives Worklynq the repositories you choose, and only those, and opens the pull request when an agent is done.

Set up GitHub →

CClaude

Works on its own branch and reports its summary, changed files and usage right on the ticket.

Set up Claude →

CCursor

Starts a Cursor cloud agent from a workflow stage. The pull request link lands on the ticket.

Set up Cursor →
  • Start by hand or by workflow. Hand a ticket over yourself, or add an agent to a stage so every ticket that reaches it goes to the agent.
  • Everything on the ticket. Status, summary, files changed, activity, the pull request and what the run used.
  • Limits you set. A maximum run time, stop-if-idle, and for Claude a maximum cost per session.
  • Your team merges. Agents never merge their own work. Every change comes back as a pull request.

Your accounts, your bill. Agent usage is billed to your own Anthropic or Cursor account, and Worklynq only reaches the repositories you grant.

AI

Your own AI

Available now

Ask about your work from the AI you already use.

Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code or Cursor to your workspace. Sign in, approve it, and ask it to find tickets, read your docs, update work or build a dashboard, right from the conversation.

  • Find and read. Search the workspace, read tickets and comments, see your stores, projects and products, and read Knowledge Center pages.
  • Get work done. Create and update tickets, move them to the next stage, comment, and write Knowledge Center pages.
  • Run the store. List orders by store or stage, see who’s on duty, and start or end your own shift.
  • Dashboards from a sentence. “Make me a dashboard of overdue work at each store.”

It works as you, with your permissions and nothing more. Untick what it may change to keep it read-only, and see everything it did in the agent activity log.

+

Custom apps

Available now

The software only your business has, in the same dashboards as everything else.

Your developers add a small, key-protected data feed to your loyalty app, ordering or booking system. Add it in Worklynq, and its data is ready for dashboards and reports. No waiting for us to build a connector.

  • Every dataset becomes a source. Customers, visits, bookings, rewards: count them, total or average them, split by status or tier, or show a trend.
  • Or just describe it. “A dashboard of loyalty members by status, with points totals.” AI builds it from your app’s real fields.
  • Works with the rest. Describe a dashboard, Explain and scheduled reports all work with your app’s data.
  • Fresh within 5 minutes. Preview what Worklynq sees, refresh after your app adds fields, or pause it anytime.

Read-only, over https, with the key stored encrypted. The feed spec asks developers to leave out personal details your dashboards don’t need.

How connections work

  • Only your System Admin connectsPeople can’t wire up company tools on their own. Apps like Claude or ChatGPT that you connect for yourself only ever act as you.
  • You choose the accessWorklynq only reaches what you allow: the repositories, Pages, locations and properties you pick.
  • Read-only where it should beBusiness systems are read-only. Worklynq never takes payments or changes your books. The one exception is Square timecards when you start or end your own shift.
  • Your account, your billConnected services run under your own account with them. AI agent usage is billed to you by Anthropic or Cursor.
  • Stored securelyKeys and tokens are stored encrypted, and Worklynq only ever shows the last few characters.
  • Disconnect anytimeOne click in the Admin console. Apps you connected yourself are disconnected from your profile menu → API tokens.

More detail in Security & data and the integration docs.
